# Scoring criteria

Levels: 1 Unusable, 3 Poor, 5 Usable, 7 Solid, 9 Polished, 10 Excellent. Use 2,
4, 6 and 8 in between.

Score only what the evidence shows. What you can't verify goes under "Could not
judge" and doesn't lower a score. A 9 needs every item in its row; otherwise
score 8 at most.

| Category | 8 | 9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hierarchy | The most important information comes first; supporting content doesn't crowd the main task. | Every page leads with its key content at every width; secondary content is clearly secondary; nothing repeats without purpose. |
| Spacing | Consistent padding and gaps; no large empty areas. | Density fits the content; even rhythm between sections; nothing cramped or padded. |
| Typography | Clear title, label, body and note levels from the type scale. | Supporting text is readable in the detail crops; nothing under the project's minimum size; labels, values and notes are easy to tell apart. |
| Consistency | Shared components and variants; the same job mostly uses the same pattern. | The same pattern does the same job everywhere (buttons, badges, cards, tables, toolbars, status, empty states, spacing, type sizes), except where decisions.md says otherwise. |
| Accessibility | Visible focus, labelled controls, no colour-only meaning in key views. | No serious or critical axe violations; every control has visible text, or an accessible name and tooltip if icon-only; accessible names contain the visible text; state is exposed (pressed, selected, expanded, sorted); headings match the visible structure; status never relies on colour alone. |
| Responsiveness | Usable at the main width without broken layouts. | Usable at every width with nothing overlapping or clipped; truncated text is available in full; wide tables scroll. |

Severity:

- **High:** blocks a task, fails WCAG A/AA, or breaks the layout at a captured
  width.
- **Medium:** a visible clarity or consistency problem in a key view. Two
  patterns for the same job is at least Medium.
- **Low:** cosmetic.
